# Follow-ups and open promises

## Unanswered sent mail

1. Search the sent folder for the period (default: last 14 days).
2. For each mail that asks something or expects an answer, check the thread (`get_thread`): a later message from the recipient counts as answered. Skip mails to colleagues that need no answer and replies that close a thread ("danke, passt").
3. List the rest with recipient, subject, date and what is outstanding.

## Open promises and deadlines

Look in the user's own sent mail for commitments with a time ("schicke ich bis Freitag", "melde mich morgen") and in received mail for deadlines set by others. Compare with today's date and with later mails in the thread. Report promises that look open or overdue, with the original wording and date.

## Limits

Answers sent from another account or by phone, and mails received only in BCC, are not visible. Say that the list may miss such cases. Nothing is marked in the mailbox to track follow-ups.

## Follow-up drafts

Only on request. Then follow `references/replies.md`: short, friendly, referring to the earlier mail, saved with `reply` and `draft: true` on the last message of the thread.
